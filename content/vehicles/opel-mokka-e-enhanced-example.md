## Overview

The Opel Mokka-e is a compact electric SUV that combines Opel's signature bold "Vizor" design with practical electric mobility. Part of the second generation Mokka lineup introduced in 2020, the fully electric variant offers an attractive package for urban and suburban drivers.

## Design Philosophy

The Mokka-e showcases Opel's new design language with the distinctive "Opel Vizor" front, which integrates the grille, headlights, and brand emblem into a single black panel. The compact dimensions make it ideal for city driving while maintaining SUV practicality.

## Technology Highlights

- **Pure Panel**: Dual 10-inch displays (digital instrument cluster + infotainment)
- **IntelliLux LED Matrix headlights**: Optional adaptive LED system with 14 elements
- **AGR-certified seats**: Ergonomically optimized front seats
- **e-Save mode**: Preserves battery for entering low-emission zones

## Real-World Usage

With a WLTP range of 338 km and real-world performance around 280 km, the Mokka-e is well-suited for daily commuting and weekend trips. The 100 kW DC fast charging capability allows for 80% charge in just 30 minutes at compatible stations.

## Market Position

Positioned as an affordable entry into the electric SUV segment, the Mokka-e competes with vehicles like the Peugeot e-2008 (platform sibling), Mazda MX-30, and Nissan Ariya (smaller variant).
